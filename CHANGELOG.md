# Changelog

## [1.0.1] — 2026-10-01

- Added Scanly custom logo and Android splash branding.
- Added gallery photo selection and crop support.
- Added photo-backed QR preview and composite PNG export.
- Removed the old landing/privacy promo panel for a cleaner phone-first UI.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-01

- Added UPI payment QR generation with optional amount and note.
- Added link and text QR generation.
- Added Leaf, Midnight, Sunset, and Ink QR templates.
- Added copy, share, and PNG export actions.
- Added offline-only privacy model with no login, cloud, database, or tracking.
